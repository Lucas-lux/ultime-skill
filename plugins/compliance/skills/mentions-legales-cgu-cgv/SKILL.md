# Mentions legales, CGU and CGV (France)

This skill lists the mandatory information and clauses. It does not produce a valid contract without
review by a lawyer: say so. Check the company's real information (registry extract, numbers) rather
than inventing it: leave fields to be completed.

## Mentions legales (legal notice, LCEN, article 6-III)

LCEN: loi pour la confiance dans l'economie numerique (French law on confidence in the digital
economy). Mandatory and accessible from every page:

- Publisher: company name or name, legal form, share capital, head office address, RCS (trade and
  companies register) or repertoire des metiers (trades register) number, intra-EU VAT number.
- Contact: email and telephone.
- Publication director (directeur de la publication).
- Host: name, company name, address, telephone.
- For a regulated activity: the professional body or authority, the professional title and the
  applicable rules.
- Additional registration numbers depending on the activity (for example a register if applicable).

## CGU (conditions generales d'utilisation, terms of use)

No general legal obligation to display them, but strongly recommended. Usual points:

- Purpose of and access to the service, account creation and management.
- Usage rules, prohibited content, moderation.
- Responsibilities and service availability.
- Intellectual property.
- Personal data: reference to the privacy policy (see `rgpd-cnil`).
- Applicable law and dispute resolution.
- For a platform with user content: hosting provider obligations (LCEN) and those of the European
  regulation on digital services (reporting, points of contact, transparency). Relevant case for a
  service with reviews and contributions. Have the scope validated by a lawyer.

## CGV (conditions generales de vente, terms of sale, Code de la consommation)

For online sales to consumers. To check in the Code de la consommation (Consumer Code):

- **Pre-contractual information**: essential characteristics, price **including all taxes**, delivery
  costs, payment and delivery terms, delivery date or lead time.
- **Right of withdrawal**: **14-day** period for a distance purchase, with the standard withdrawal form
  provided and the legal exceptions (personalized goods, digital content supplied with consent and
  waiver...). Inform clearly, otherwise the period is extended.
- **Legal warranties**: legal warranty of conformity and warranty against hidden defects, presented
  separately from any commercial warranty.
- **Order button**: the order confirmation that entails an obligation to pay carries a clear mention
  such as "commande avec obligation de paiement" (order with obligation to pay).
- **Consumer mediator**: contact details and information on the European Union online dispute
  resolution platform.
- **Online cancellation**: for a contract taken out online, offer a simple and direct online
  cancellation route ("resiliation en trois clics", cancellation in three clicks).
- **Prices**: prices shown including all taxes (TTC), and where applicable the information on the
  previous price in case of a reduction.

## Method

- Produce templates with the mandatory sections and fields to be completed (identity, host, mediator,
  actual lead times), never invented information.
- Remind that the texts must be validated by a lawyer and dated.
- For personal data and cookies, refer to the skills `rgpd-cnil` and `cookies-consentement-cnil`.
- Check every figure or time limit at the source before publication.
