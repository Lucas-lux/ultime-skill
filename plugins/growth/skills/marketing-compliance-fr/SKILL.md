# Marketing compliance (France and EU)

Gives the requirements and the references; the texts change: check the version in force on Legifrance
or EUR-Lex, and have a lawyer validate sensitive cases.

## Influencer marketing (influence commerciale)

- Loi n° 2023-451 du 9 juin 2023 (Law No. 2023-451 of 9 June 2023): clear mention « Publicite » or
  « Collaboration commerciale » throughout the content; mentions « Image retouchee » (retouched image)
  or « Image virtuelle » (virtual image) for modified or generated images that depict a person; written
  contract between the brand and the creator above the thresholds set by decree.
- Some promotions are banned or regulated (notably cosmetic surgery, nicotine products, gambling,
  financial products and crypto-assets): check before briefing.
- The advertiser shares responsibility for the creator's breaches.

## Prospecting

- Email and SMS to individuals: prior consent, except for an existing customer for similar products or
  services; to professionals, possible without consent if the message relates to their profession,
  with a right to object (article L34-5 of the Code des postes et des communications electroniques
  (Post and Electronic Communications Code), CNIL doctrine).
- Every message: sender identity and simple, free unsubscription.
- Telephone: specific regime (opt-out list, recent legislative changes) to check before any calling
  campaign.

## Trackers and advertising measurement

- Advertising pixels and SDKs (Meta, TikTok, Google Ads...): prior consent (skill
  `cookies-consentement-cnil`). Google requires Consent Mode for ad measurement and personalization for
  users in the European Economic Area.

## Message and price

- Misleading or aggressive commercial practices are banned (Code de la consommation (Consumer Code),
  articles L121-1 and following); comparative advertising is regulated (L122-1 and following).
- Price reduction announcement: the reference price is the lowest price applied in the previous 30
  days (L112-1-1).
- Online reviews: fake reviews banned; inform on how reviews are collected and checked (L111-7-2).
- Contests: accessible rules, clear participation conditions.
- Advertising broadcast in France written in French (loi n° 94-665 du 4 aout 1994, Law No. 94-665 of 4
  August 1994), with translations of foreign terms.

## Regulated sectors

Alcohol (Loi Evin), health and medicines, food supplements and claims, gambling (ANJ), credit
(mandatory mentions), food (health messages), financial services. ARPP ethical recommendations for all
sectors.

## Targeting and platforms

- Digital Services Act: no targeted advertising based on profiling using sensitive data, nor
  profiling-based advertising to minors; set audiences accordingly.
- Each platform's advertising policies (special categories: credit, employment, housing, politics,
  health): read them before creating.

## AI-generated content

- AI Act (article 50): disclose deepfakes (realistic images, sounds, videos that are generated or
  manipulated).
- AI labels required by the platforms; mention « Image virtuelle » in influencer marketing.

## Checklist before publication

Advertising and partnership mentions, consents (prospecting, trackers), justified prices and
reductions, substantiated claims, rights on visuals and music, compliant targeting, AI content
disclosed, French language, legal validation if regulated sector.
